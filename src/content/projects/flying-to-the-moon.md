overview:
  - Countries around the world are embracing the challenges of space exploration. In recent years, various nations have successively introduced plans for lunar exploration, and research facilities are expected to beestablished in the near future.
  - This project summarizes the requirements of research bases to form modular individual buildings, and establishes architectural generation rules based on the functional workflows of typical research buildings. Ultimately,the final design is produced through a wave function collapse algorithm.
