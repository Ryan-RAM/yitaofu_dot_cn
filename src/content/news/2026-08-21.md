Like the first line of a new program, yitaofu.cn begins with a simple message: Hello World. From this starting point, the website has grown into a personal platform for documenting my projects, research interests, computational experiments, and ongoing exploration at the intersection of architecture, computation, and emerging technologies.

More than a portfolio, the website is intended to become an evolving archive of my architectural development, recording the evolution of ideas, design methods, and perspectives throughout my academic and professional journey. It represents the beginning of a more intentional effort to document, reflect on, and share my architectural practice publicly.
