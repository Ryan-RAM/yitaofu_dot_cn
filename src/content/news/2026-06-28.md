I officially completed my undergraduate education at Guangdong University of Technology, receiving both a Bachelor of Architecture degree and a Bachelor of Engineering degree.

Over five years of study, I pursued Architecture as my major and Computer Science as my minor. This interdisciplinary background allowed me to develop two complementary perspectives: architecture cultivated spatial imagination and design thinking, while computer science strengthened my understanding of logic and systematic problem-solving.

The combination of these two disciplines gradually shaped my interest in the intersection of architecture and technology, particularly in exploring how computational methods can expand the possibilities of architectural design.

This milestone marks the conclusion of my undergraduate architectural education at Guangdong University of Technology and the beginning of the next stage of my education at Columbia GSAPP.
